# CodeAlpha - Unemployment Analysis with Python

## Project Overview
This project is part of my **CodeAlpha Data Science Internship**.
The project focuses on analyzing unemployment rate data in India using Python. The analysis includes data cleaning, regional analysis, monthly trends, COVID-19 impact analysis, and visualization.

## Objectives
- Analyze unemployment rates in India
- Clean and prepare the dataset
- Compare unemployment rates across regions
- Analyze monthly unemployment trends
- Study the impact of COVID-19 on unemployment
- Identify high and low unemployment periods
- Provide useful policy insights

## Dataset
The project uses two datasets:

### 1. Unemployment in India
This dataset contains unemployment information across different regions of India.
Important columns include:
- Region
- Date
- Frequency
- Estimated Unemployment Rate (%)
- Estimated Employed
- Estimated Labour Participation Rate (%)
- Area

### 2. Unemployment Rate During COVID-19
This dataset is used to analyze unemployment during the COVID-19 period.
It contains information such as:
- Region
- Date
- Estimated Unemployment Rate (%)
- Estimated Employed
- Estimated Labour Participation Rate (%)
- Area

## Technologies Used
- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

## Project Steps

### 1. Data Loading
The unemployment datasets were loaded using Pandas.

### 2. Data Cleaning
The data was checked for:
- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Incorrect column formatting
- Date format
Missing values and duplicate records were removed.

### 3. Regional Analysis
The average unemployment rate was calculated for different regions of India.
A bar chart was created to compare unemployment rates across regions.

### 4. Unemployment Trend Analysis
The unemployment rate was analyzed over time to identify changes and trends.

### 5. COVID-19 Analysis
The second dataset was used to analyze unemployment during the COVID-19 period.
A line chart was created to visualize the changes in unemployment rates.

### 6. Monthly Analysis
The unemployment rate was analyzed month-wise to identify periods with higher and lower unemployment.

## Key Findings
- The average unemployment rate was approximately **11.79%**.
- Unemployment rates varied significantly across different regions.
- The COVID-19 period showed a significant increase in unemployment.
- **May** had the highest average monthly unemployment rate at approximately **24.52%**.
- **November** had the lowest average monthly unemployment rate at approximately **8.14%**.
- Tripura, Haryana, Jharkhand and Bihar were among the regions with relatively higher average unemployment rates.

## Visualizations
The project includes visualizations for:
- Average Unemployment Rate by Region
- Unemployment Rate Trend
- Unemployment Rate During COVID-19 Period
- Monthly Unemployment Rate Pattern

## Policy Insights
- Regions with higher unemployment rates may need targeted employment-generation programs.
- Skill development and training programs can be focused on areas with higher unemployment.
- The changes observed during the COVID-19 period show the importance of emergency employment and financial support during major economic disruptions.
- Monthly variations can help policymakers plan employment-support programs during periods of higher unemployment.
- Rural and urban areas can be considered separately when designing employment policies.

## Conclusion
The analysis identified differences in unemployment rates across regions and months and showed significant changes during the COVID-19 period.
These findings can help policymakers focus on employment generation, skill development, and financial support programs for highly affected regions and periods.
